# Jarvis /welcome: brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The product facts, honesty rules and constraints come from the task; the creative decisions below are authored. No interview. No generated assets: CSS, SVG and real markup only.

1. **Vibe:** a lamp, a desk, an account book, a red pencil. References: an auditor's working papers; a ship's log; the quiet of a night shift.
2. **Journey:** the desk at night, the book lands flat, what memory leaves out, three ways trades get in, one trade checked by hand, what the record shows, the coach, what it can and can't do, the next line is yours.
3. **Energy:** calm, then a sting, practical, silent, then the long held peak, then open, intimate, flat and plain, then stop.
4. **Feeling and moment:** see curve and peak.
5. **The thing no other site does:** the page does the sum in pencil under your hand, then the app's printed figure lands on the pencil one.
6. **Range:** premium dark, with one hard cut to paper. Not gold. Ledger green-black, bone, one red-pencil accent.
7. **World:** one object (the book), distinct folios. Not a continuous flight.
8. **Assets:** none. Everything drawn.

**Believe by the end:** the record is mine, it stays on my machine, and its figures can be checked.
**Action:** "Open Jarvis", to /login.

## Grammar: "The working book" (new)

The page is an open account book. Unit: the folio on a ruled ground, with a red margin rule held at one x position for the whole page. Nav: an index in the margin that ticks entries as you pass them. Hero: a physical book on a desk that lands flat and becomes the page. Ending: an accountant's double rule under the last line, with the CTA as that line.
Bans: `scrub`, `spotlight`, `magnet`, `drift`, centred acts, stat counters, feature-card grids, media under running type.
Why not the eight: filmic has no way to say "check this"; editorial's title page forbids the depth hero; live surface would need the whole app running on a public page; continuous world has no real geography; poster gives no room for product; gallery is not a range; split stage strains across six features; cutlist is the wrong pulse for a calm tool.

## Feeling curve

1. Calm intimacy: desk, lamp pool, red pencil, the book tilts up and lands flat under your scroll.
2. A small sting: "You remember the trades you were right about", lines darkening in as you read.
3. Practical relief: three plain ruled entries wipe in, one per way in.
4. Held breath (authored silence, not dead scroll): one line on an almost empty viewport.
5. Satisfaction, the peak: pencil writes the sum, printed figure drops onto it, red tick.
6. Curiosity: calendar, analytics, notes travel sideways.
7. Intimacy: a short exchange with the coach about your own record.
8. Steadiness: hard cut to paper. Plain statements of limits.
9. Resolve: one line, double rule, stop.

**Peak (act 5, largest span):** "it worked out a losing trade in pencil as I scrolled, and then the app's number dropped exactly onto the pencil one."
**Tell someone:** it's the site where a trade gets worked out by hand while you scroll, and the app's printed answer lands on top of it, digit for digit.
